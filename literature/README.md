# literature/

`READING_LIST.md`: one entry per paper (what it gives us, where the T-03 memo uses it) and, at the end, a DOI or arXiv link for each.

PDFs are **not tracked** (`.gitignore`: `literature/*.pdf`), except our own manuscript `Large Prior.pdf`. Keep your local copies in this folder; they stay on disk. To re-fetch a PDF, use the link in `READING_LIST.md`. This keeps third-party copyrighted files out of the repository.
