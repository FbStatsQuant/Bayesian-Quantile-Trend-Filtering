## 1. The candidate: exact augmented Gibbs (individual) + joint v3 (exact reflective HMC)
- **Target identity.**
  - The individual sampler targets exactly his posterior: the ALD working likelihood (Kozumi–Kobayashi z), the Laplace prior on D³θ as a normal scale mixture, λ ~ Gamma(1, b₀), σ ~ IG(0.1, 0.1), raw scale, and his per-signal b₀.
  - Validated by E-04, by agreement with his chain wherever his chain mixes (G0), and by σ agreeing with his chains at n = 500 (median ratio 1.000).
  - The joint v3 targets his joint posterior: the product of the per-level posteriors restricted to θ₁ ≤ … ≤ θ_J, using his joint priors from his package configs (§4).
- **Algorithm.**
  - **Individual:** an exact banded Gaussian θ draw (stacked banded QR, O(n)), clamp-free exact z and 1/s draws, σ | θ with z integrated out, a conjugate λ draw, and a collapsed (λ, θ) block (Knorr-Held & Rue). Stopping rule: 4 chains, rank-normalised bulk and folded R̂ ≤ 1.01, bulk and tail ESS ≥ 400 (`posterior` package).
  - **Joint:** per level, θ_j | rest is a box-truncated Gaussian, drawn by exact reflective HMC (Pakman & Paninski 2014). The trajectory and wall reflections are closed form, with no accept step and no bounce cap. Its exactness against rejection sampling is verified (`joint_gibbs/RESULTS_v3.md`).
