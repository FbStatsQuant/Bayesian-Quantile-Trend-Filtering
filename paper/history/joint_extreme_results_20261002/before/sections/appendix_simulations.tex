\section{Additional Simulation Results}
\label{app:simulation_results}

This appendix reports the MAD and MCIW results accompanying the VS and S interior-quantile MSE tables in Section~\ref{sec:sim:individual}. All tables use the $n=500$ comparison.

\subsection{Interior quantiles}
\label{app:sim:individual}

This appendix reports the measures omitted from Section~\ref{sec:sim:individual}: MAD and MCIW for the varying-smoothness signal, and the complete set of MSE, MAD and MCIW tables for the smooth signal. All entries use the default order $k=2$ and $n=500$, and are averages over valid replications. MCIW is the average width of the $95\%$ pointwise posterior intervals and is not available for the ADMM point estimator.

\subsubsection{Varying smoothness}
\label{app:sim:individual:vs}
The MAD comparison (Table~\ref{tab:vs_int_mad}) agrees with the MSE comparison of Section~\ref{sec:sim:ind:vs}. Bayesian-PMALA's intervals are narrower than those of both Onizuka variants (Table~\ref{tab:vs_int_mciw}); the widths describe different posterior constructions and are not by themselves a measure of calibration.
