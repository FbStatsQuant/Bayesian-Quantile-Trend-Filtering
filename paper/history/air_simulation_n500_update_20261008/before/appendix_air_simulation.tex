\section{Additional results for the simulated air-quality experiment}
\label{app:air_simulation}

Tables~\ref{tab:air_n500}, \ref{tab:air_n2000}, and \ref{tab:air_n4000} give the remaining sample sizes for the experiment in Section~\ref{sec:air_simulation}. They use the same posterior-mean summaries, independently fitted ADMM benchmark, and replication-wise averaging as Table~\ref{tab:air_n1000}. Undefined CAA values are omitted. CL and MSE are smaller-is-better measures; larger CAA indicates better classification.

Three further tables complete the record. Table~\ref{tab:air_mse_se} gives the standard error of each method's mean MSE across replications and, since the three methods fit identical datasets, the paired differences between Bayesian-PMALA and each competitor. Table~\ref{tab:air_diagnostics} collects the finite-chain diagnostics of the two samplers and the coverage and width of their pointwise $95\%$ bands for the target \eqref{eq:air_target}. Table~\ref{tab:air_penalty} records the penalty levels reached by Bayesian-PMALA under the prior \eqref{eq:air_prior} and by the eBIC selection, together with the number of ADMM fits whose selected penalty was the largest grid value.
