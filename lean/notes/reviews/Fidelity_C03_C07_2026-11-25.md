**(b) Mathematics.** Put \(a=\min\{\tau,1-\tau\}>0\), \(\theta=Nc+Su\), and \(v=y-Nc-Su\). The check loss satisfies \(\sum_i\rho_\tau(v_i)\ge a\|v\|_1\), and orthonormality of the columns of \(N\) gives
\[
\|v\|_1\ge\|v\|_2\ge\|N^\top v\|_2=\|c-N^\top(y-Su)\|_2.
\]
For each fixed \(u\), translation of \(c\) therefore bounds the integrated likelihood by \(\int_{\mathbb R^r}e^{-a\|c\|_2}\,dc=r!V_r/a^r<\infty\), using radial integration. The bound is independent of \(u\). Tonelli against the probability measure \(Q_\lambda\) and multiplication by \(\exp\{\sum_i\rho_\tau(Z_i)\}\) give \(N(\mathbb R^n)<\infty\). The relative likelihood is strictly positive everywhere, and the product measure gives \([0,1]^r\times\mathbb R^m\) mass one, so its integral is strictly positive. **(b) Mathematics.** For \(t>0\) and \(0\le z<t\), integration of \(2(t-s)\) from \(z\) to \(t\) gives \((t-z)^2\). For \(t<0\) and \(t<z<0\), integration of \(2(s-t)\) from \(t\) to \(z\) gives \((z-t)^2\). In either case, outside the stated range of \(z\) both expressions for the squared remainder vanish. Assumption B gives \(F_i(0)=F_i(0-)=\tau\). For \(0<s\le t\le L\), the positive-shift indicator has expectation \(\Pr(0\le Z_i<s)\le F_i(s)-\tau\le\bar f s\). For \(-L\le t\le s<0\), the negative-shift indicator has expectation \(\Pr(s<Z_i<0)=\tau-F_i(s)\le\bar f(-s)\). Applying Tonelli gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb E R_i(t)^2 &\le \bar f\int_0^t 2(t-s)s\,ds=\bar f t^3/3 &&(0<t\le L),\\
\mathbb E R_i(t)^2 &\le \bar f\int_t^0 2(s-t)(-s)\,ds=\bar f(-t)^3/3 &&(-L\le t<0).
\end{aligned}
\]
At \(t=0\), \(R_i(0)=0\). For \(|t|\le\min\{L,3\underline f/(4\bar f)\}\), part (d) gives \(m_i(t)=\mathbb E R_i(t)\ge\underline f t^2/2\). Use \(e^{-x}\le1-x+x^2/2\) with \(x=2R_i(t)\ge0\). The quadratic term is bounded by \(2\bar f |t|^3/3\le\underline f t^2/2\le m_i(t)\), so \(\mathbb E e^{-2R_i(t)}\le1-m_i(t)\le e^{-m_i(t)}\).
