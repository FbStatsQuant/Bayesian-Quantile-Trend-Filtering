## Candidate paper corrections

- **Incorrect wording in the finiteness argument.** The translated upper bound for the `c`-integral is independent of `u`. The integral itself generally varies with `u`. For `n = 2`, `r = 1`, `y = θ* = 0`, and `τ = 1/2`, take `N = (1,1)ᵀ/√2` and `S = (0,1)ᵀ`. The integral is `√2 (|u| + 2) exp(−|u|/2)`. State the uniform upper bound or use the direct product envelope from the Lean proof. Strict positivity of the integrand on a set of positive measure gives the other normalizer inequality. [appendix_theory.tex:83](/home/fb31/.t3/worktrees/Bayesian-Quantile-Trend-Filtering/review-neonA-7c61aea/paper/sections/appendix_theory.tex:83)
- **Boundary case in the interpolation proof.** The window argument cites the coordinate lemma for `w ≥ r`, but the cited lemma assumes `w > r`. For `w = r`, polynomial evaluations of degree at most `r − 1` span the whole window by interpolation at its `r` distinct points. Take `p = δ_W` and the difference vector empty. This supplies the missing boundary case with zero error. [appendix_theory.tex:221](/home/fb31/.t3/worktrees/Bayesian-Quantile-Trend-Filtering/review-neonA-7c61aea/paper/sections/appendix_theory.tex:221)
- **Optional change-of-variables detail.** Write `dθ = |det [N S]| dc du` in the Lebesgue posterior and cancel this common factor along with the Laplace normalizing constant and the reference loss factor. The paper already invokes the constant Jacobian, so the explicit cancellation is an exposition improvement. [appendix_theory.tex:83](/home/fb31/.t3/worktrees/Bayesian-Quantile-Trend-Filtering/review-neonA-7c61aea/paper/sections/appendix_theory.tex:83)

To obtain the last claim from the displayed formulas, use Pythagoras to bound the projected radius, then apply the volume identity with `H = 1` and use `Q_λ(ℝᵐ) = 1`.
