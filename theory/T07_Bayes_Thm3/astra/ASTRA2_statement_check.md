# Review of the T07a statement and Padilla proof map

S denotes `T07a_statement.tex` and D denotes `PADILLA_THM3_BREAKDOWN.md`. Line numbers refer to the reviewed versions. The fixed-scale Bayesian upper rate survives these checks. Two steps supporting the separate mode corollary fail under the hypotheses printed in the source.

1. **ERROR — A quantile need not center Padilla's score.** S 84–89, 117–129, 228 and D 8, 51, 58, 72–75, 94–95. Confidence high. Take the median zero, with CDF on the left given by F(−t)=1/2−t²/2 for 0<t≤1, an atom of mass 1/4 at zero, and the remaining 1/4 uniform on (0,1). A holds with L=1 and lower slope 1/4. Yet E u=1/2−F(0)=−1/4 in (35), and the expected excess at −t is ∫₀ᵗ[1/2−F(−s)]ds=t³/6. Its ratio to Δ²(−t)=t² tends to zero, contradicting Lemma 13. Translations preserve this example at any sequence of quantiles, including truths with V*=1. The proof invokes A explicitly in Lemma 13, but both (19) and (35) use the extra identity F(q)=τ. Fix the mode argument by requiring that identity (continuity at q suffices) and addressing finding 2. B already supplies it for the main theorem by taking δ↑0. The project's L2 proof independently records this issue.

2. **ERROR — Lemma 24 fails on the raw difference ball, even for continuous median noise.** D 55–56, 59–60, 66 and S 129, 227. Confidence high. Set r=2, n=2m+1, i=−m,…,m and xᵢ=(|i|−m(m+1)/n)/2. Symmetry and centering make x orthogonal to both constants and i, hence x is in the row space. Its only nonzero second difference is 1. On a fixed fraction of the grid |xᵢ| is of order n, and everywhere it is O(n). Thus ‖x‖₂≍n^{3/2}, Δ(x)≍n, and Lemma 24's claimed uniform lower comparison of Δ to ‖·‖₂ fails. This also refutes the probabilistic lemma itself. Set V*=1 and use independent fair scores ±1/2. Lemma 23's constants give A≍n^{6/5}(log n)^{1/5} and B̃≍n^{1/10}(log n)^{1/10}. The supremum in Lemma 24 includes both signs of x. By the weighted CLT (largest weight divided by ‖x‖₂ is O(n^{-1/2})), |uᵀx| exceeds a fixed multiple of n^{3/2} with probability bounded away from zero. Consequently (|uᵀx|−A)/Δ(x) exceeds a multiple of n^{1/2} there. This rules out O_pr(B̃). The same example violates Lemma 19 with V=n. Fix the review by flagging the gap at (37) and Proposition 3. A proof using a correctly scaled, localized noise bound is needed before certifying the corollary. This example refutes a proof input and leaves the continuous-noise PQTF rate open in this review. Use project C1/W′ for Bayesian localization.

3. **OK — Both tuning conversions, the loss, propriety and the MAP identity.** S 48–80, 103–126, 133–146 and D 16–19, 33–47. Confidence high. Coefficient matching gives λ_raw=n^{r−1}λ_TV. Adding r−1 to 1/(2r+1) gives r(2r−1)/(2r+1), and adding it to 1/2 gives (2r−1)/2. At r=3 these are 15/7 and 5/2. Squaring (46) and dividing by n gives precisely the stated Δ²_n rate, since Δ²=nΔ²_n. One coordinate of the unnormalized likelihood integrates over θᵢ to σ/τ+σ/(1−τ). The full positive posterior kernel is bounded above by the product of these integrable likelihood factors. Multiplying its negative log by σ gives S(θ)+σλ_TV TV(θ), proving equality of the entire sets of optimizers, including ties. Retain these identities.

6. **IMPRECISION — B is a curvature condition for this evidence proof.** S 91–98, 226, 237 and D 10, 103. Confidence high. With F(q)=τ the mean excess is ∫₀ᵈ[F(q+s)−τ]ds ≤ f̄ d²/2 locally under B. For |d|>L the Lipschitz bound |d|≤d²/L extends this to an empirical ℓ₂ ball. Conversely a continuous CDF with F(q+s)−τ proportional to sign(s)√|s| near zero satisfies A but has excess proportional to |d|^{3/2}, precluding this uniform quadratic estimate. B controls increments anchored at q. It allows, for example, adding small atoms accumulating at distances 2^{-j} with masses proportional to 2^{-j} to a smooth positive-density component, with q chosen as the quantile at the accumulation point. Their total mass within distance h is O(h). Thus A+B need not give a density throughout a neighborhood. Describe B's use in L6 and in exact score centering/C2. Necessity for every possible evidence argument or for contraction itself is unproved.

10. **OK — The heuristic spread and bias balance in grid units.** S 179–185 and D 115–121. Confidence high for the algebra. A window of w grid points has prior standard deviation proportional to (Σ_{j≤w} j^{2r−2})^{1/2}/λ≍w^{r−1/2}/λ. Equating to w^{-1/2} gives w≍λ^{1/r} and squared spread 1/w. A localized kink of strength V in the (r−1)st derivative has amplitude of order V(w/n)^{r−1} over fraction w/n of the grid, giving squared approximation cost of order V²(w/n)^{2r−1}. Equating that expression with 1/w gives λ²=n^{2r−1}/V² and the stated polynomial rate. Use this as a worst-case smoothing heuristic for V>0 in the regime 1≪w≪n. A polynomial truth has zero approximation cost. Similarly S 238 and D 109 describe the obstruction for a fixed positive class bound V, whereas V*_n=O(1) itself may vanish.
