# Independent referee report on Theorem DC-W, §§8–9

**Verdict — ACCEPT WITH FIXES.** The realized-density comparison and weighted spectral gain prove the bounded-tuning evidence result for the listed designs. The theorem statement needs the corner margin for the particular γ being claimed. The literal Beta interval [1.96, 4.1] survives at total slack 0.03. Withdraw the purported upper bound on all refinements.

1. **CONFIRMED — Evidence normalization and projection (§8.2, previous findings 1–2).** The substitution contributes det(W) to the density and integration over flat coordinates contributes 1/|det G|, with GᵀG = NᵀW²N. Use the fiber-integral version of the quotient density for the pointwise identity. Put H = DΣ¹ᐟ². Since HWS = I, orthogonally projecting WS off ker H gives H⁺, the unique right inverse with columns in (ker H)⊥. Its Gram operator is (HᵀH)⁺. This proves the E3 determinant has the claimed eigenvalues even for nonconstant variances. E3 is deterministic for every s. At a fixed tuning pair, the conditional likelihood ratio in E2 has conditional expectation at most one since integration is over the true support. Reserve simultaneity of that event for the grid proof.

2. **CONFIRMED — E5 and the realized projected density (§8.4).** On the true support E[g(T)/f_T(T)] ≤ 1, including for bounded-support laws. The Gaussian reference centered at the *actual mean* incurs quadratic cost ε̃_cᵀΠε̃_c/2. Independence gives variance 2 tr(Π²) + Σᵢ(E ε̃_c,i⁴ − 3)Πᵢᵢ² ≤ max(2,m̃₄)d before division by four. Markov and Chebyshev give the displayed result without independence between those events. Use one such event per distinct deterministic projection. No distributional approximation is needed.

3. **CONFIRMED — E4, including both tail cases (§8.3).** Tonelli and the translated density ratio bound the expected integral inside the deterministic ball by its volume. For bounded supports, membership of both observations in the same translated interval implies ‖b‖ ≤ √n S̄/ς_min, regardless of the interval locations. For the envelope proof, center Chebyshev at the finite-sample sum of whitened entropies. Use H₀ = supₙ |h̄ₙ − n⁻¹Σ log ςᵢ| < ∞ in the radius and replace log C̃₀ by its positive part. The radial tail is a constant times (1+B_n²)exp(−c̃₀ B_n), so the extra n in the radius absorbs the polynomial. With B_n = O(Kn), the log overhead exhibited by this proof is **3 log n + 4 log K + O(1)**, since the inner integral costs K times the ball volume. Replace the displayed 3 log(Kn)+C by this bound or by O(log(Kn)). Proposition N-W already allows that error. Entropy concentration for the final density upper bound uses the opposite tail from that used to set the radius.

4. **CONFIRMED — Weighted spectral gain (§8.5, previous finding 3).** Keep only stencils lying wholly within each block. The principal submatrix is block diagonal, and interlacing followed by Loewner monotonicity bounds each ascending full eigenvalue from above by its comparator. For decreasing ϕ̂, the first d comparator terms dominate the selected d_b from each block. Half the sum on block b is at least the integral of 3 log(x*_b/z) from 4 to x*_b, namely 3x*_b − 12 − 12 log(x*_b/4). Thus the final equality with the log n remainder should be a **lower bound** with remainder −O(B log n). Use balanced consecutive blocks and compare the empirical average of v⁻¹ᐟ⁶ first to its block minima and then to its integral. The former error is at most (1/B+1/n)TV(v⁻¹ᐟ⁶), the latter at most TV(v⁻¹ᐟ⁶)/n. The sign of Ā−Ā_B need not be positive at finite n when the maxima use sampled sites. Take B large and fixed, then n large. Include 6 ≤ x*_b ≤ n_b−3 at the nodes, or freeze the projection chosen at an admissible ℓ₀ and use its improving E3 bound for ℓ ≤ ℓ₀. The sufficient asymptotic lower-end condition ℓ₁ς_min > (cΛ/3)³ holds in the table.

5. **CONFIRMED — Uniformity and theorem hypotheses (§8.6, previous findings 4–7).** Specify fixed 0 < ℓ₁ < ℓ₀, positive γ,η,δ, and **γ < log(2tR/σ₀)** for this proof. The printed hypothesis σ₀ < 2tR guarantees some positive γ but does not impose this inequality on a prescribed γ. The stronger σ₀ exp(2γ) < 2tR suffices and is the condition for the earlier interpolating-fit argument. At small scales take a common positive s_min with 8ℓ₀s_min < min(τ,1−τ) and log Mτ(8ℓ₀s_min) below the remaining corner margin. At large scales maximize s³⁻ⁿ at s_hi. On the closed rectangle use an endpoint-inclusive log grid and bound from the node with **larger s and smaller ℓ**, at cost ≤ 2δ. A direct union bound N(exp(−ηn)+exp(−log²n)+5/log n) suffices, without counting shared events. Choose B using the strict surplus in the spectral inequality. The stochastic node error is O(√(log n/n)) after division by n, while the deterministic entropy convergence in (M2) has no specified rate. Reserve a positive margin in all three regions for literal DC_γ. For the posterior conclusion also impose the benchmark denominator hypotheses, including (A3). To join region (I), start the compact proof at a fixed value below its slack-adjusted cutoff, whose limit is (1−η′)exp(−η′)2t/(eσ₀) for that argument's slack η′. The nominal table value alone does not prove the join. Restrict design conclusions to verified levels.

6. **CONFIRMED — Design hypotheses and corner margins (§8.7).** Beta has v = b/[(1+b)²(2+b)] and varentropy (1−1/b)². For Mixed the density lies between exp(−(|x|+0.2)²)/√π and 1/√π, giving a uniform second moment of the information by the component fourth moments. The stated Gaussian upper envelope supplies (M3) after the bounded quantile shift at fixed τ. In the stencil average, three-dependence and bounded second moments give variance O(1/n). Smoothly varying design laws converge locally to four independent draws from the same law, giving the stated integral for R by uniform integrability and Riemann summation. The deterministic signal contribution is o(1) under the stated third-difference condition. The ratios in noFloor_necessary.out use simulation for R. A deterministic margin check follows by retaining the two middle stencil terms: conditional Jensen gives R ≥ 3∫ E|Y−Y′| du for independent local copies. In Beta, the survival function gives E|Y−Y′| = 2b/[(1+b)(1+2b)], hence R ≥ 0.46399 (rounded). In Mixed, conditional on the labels the difference is Gaussian of variance one, whose mean absolute value is minimized at mean zero, so R ≥ 3√(2/π). Both bounds cover even γ = 0.03 with the stronger corner inequality at the tabulated levels.
