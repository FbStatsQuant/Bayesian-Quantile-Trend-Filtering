# Independent referee report on FABLE_B_L2_analysis, Parts 1 and 2

**Verdict — ACCEPT WITH FIXES for the bounded-range result at the tabulated Gaussian levels.** The projection proof works, including throughout the intermediate scale band. Withdraw the unrestricted “at every τ” conclusion, distinguish the evidence and posterior hypotheses, and correct the endpoint rounding. No new growing-tuning result follows.

1. **CONFIRMED — Evidence identity and normalization (§2.1).** With orthonormal columns of N and orthonormal rows of Q, the map from y to (Nᵀy,Qy) has absolute Jacobian one. For fixed u,E the admissible c form a translate of A, of volume |A|. Tonelli therefore identifies the quotient density with tⁿZ. Taking the density version defined by that integral gives the identity at every y. The determinant Jₙ occurs only on changing from orthonormal quotient coordinates to Dy. No repair needed.

2. **CONFIRMED — Conditional comparison and projection (§§2.2–2.3).** Under the Gaussian truth the conditional density is positive, and for fixed tuning the model-to-truth conditional density ratio has conditional mean one given Py. Markov thus costs eᵃ with failure probability at most e⁻ᵃ. For the first d positive-frequency eigenvectors ordered by increasing μ, PS=PD⁺ and (PS)(PS)ᵀ has diagonal entries 1/μᵣ in this basis. E3 and independent convolution give log‖π_T‖∞ ≤ −½Σ log[4/(ℓ²μᵣ)] for every s. E3's exponential negative moment and log-Gamma chord give exactly the stated determinant factor. **Fix the quantifiers** in the remark after Proposition N. The deterministic projection bound holds simultaneously. The Markov event is pointwise in tuning until the grid argument.

3. **CONFIRMED — Proposition N, with two qualifications (§3).** The Schur determinant identity is correct, and the two Gaussian quadratic forms have the stated marginal chi-square laws without an independence requirement. Subtracting the projected entropy cost gives the summand ½[log(2/(ℓ²ς_max²μᵣ))−1−log π] as a lower bound on the gain. Use an inequality in (iii). Lemma 2a gives at least 3 log(x*/(r+3)) per retained mode. Since this function decreases to zero, summing from 4 to floor(x*) dominates its integral from 4 to x*, which is 3x*−12−12 log(x*/4). Thus 3x*/n = cΛ(ℓς_max)⁻¹ᐟ³ with cΛ = 0.7497255685… . The printed 0.7497 rounds safely down. Keep 6 ≤ x* ≤ n−3 as a hypothesis of each application. For a fixed interval a sufficient lower endpoint condition is ℓ₁ς_max > (cΛ/3)³, satisfied by all tabulated endpoints. Alternatively use the same projection chosen at an admissible ℓ₀ for every smaller ℓ, since its E3 bound improves as ℓ decreases. The additive constant in (i) can be taken as (3/2)log(2πe)+3log ς_max. Allow dependence on the absolute scale, since rescaling all standard deviations preserves their ratio.

4. **CONFIRMED — Uniform scale coverage (§4 and DC §§2.1–2.3).** The flat integral gives Z ≤ 8πk⁻³s³⁻ⁿ. Take its supremum over s ≥ s_hi at s_hi for n>3. Dropping 3 log s/n as a uniform o(1) over this half-line would be invalid. At small s the column-sum bound ‖DE‖₁ ≤ 8‖E‖₁ gives the claimed log Mτ(8ℓs) correction for 8ℓs<k. Write G=log(2tR/σ₀) and take γ<G. Choose a fixed s_min with 8ℓ₀s_min<k and log Mτ(8ℓ₀s_min)<(G−γ)/2. The uniform replacement error from Rₙ is bounded by ℓ₀|Rₙ−R|. On the remaining closed rectangle use a fixed endpoint-inclusive log grid, bounding each point from the node with **larger s and smaller ℓ** at cost ≤2δ. A finite union of the node failure probabilities tends to zero. These three sets cover all s, including [0.06/ℓ,1/ℓ]. Reserve strict slack or report any slightly smaller γ to obtain the literal (DC_γ) inequality from a bound with +o(1).

5. **CONFIRMED — Limits of the Gaussian claim (§§0, 1, 4).** For τ≠½ use the true conditional quantile as the location in computing σ₀. In quantile-centered coordinates the residual is ςᵢ(Zᵢ−zτ), not a centered Gaussian. All density calculations survive the deterministic mean shift. For the paper's smooth scale profile the normalized third-difference norm of that shift vanishes. In the corner calculation, for independent centered Gaussians the exact expectation of Rₙ for zero signal is √(2/π) times the sum over stencils of √(ςⱼ²+9ςⱼ₊₁²+9ςⱼ₊₂²+ςⱼ₊₃²), divided by n. Bounded variances and three-dependence give variance O(1/n). The quoted simplification to √(40/π) mean(ς) holds for the smooth paper design. For general heteroscedastic sequences use the actual stencil means and account for any deterministic shift. A signal with ‖Dθ*‖₁=o(n) changes Rₙ by o(1).

   Even in the paper design, σ₀/(2tR)=φ(zτ)/[τ(1−τ)√(160/π)] diverges as τ↓0 by Mills' ratio. At τ=10⁻¹⁶ it is about 1.169. Indeed, taking s↓0 and ℓ=1/R gives limiting per-site log evidence −1−log(2tR), **above the benchmark** when σ₀>2tR. This directly defeats the all-tuning corner argument. It defeats DC on an interval containing 1/R under those conditions. Separately, at τ=10⁻⁸ the stated 0.03-slack formula gives ℓ₀≈0.314 below the nominal ℓ₁≈0.382. State DC-G for a specified nonempty interval meeting the projection and margin hypotheses. None of these objections affects the listed paper levels. For the posterior consequence, cite the benchmark lower bound with its own truth/prior-concentration hypotheses. The denominator proof quoted in the A-rule note uses the bounded variation budget V at the prescribed scaling. A numerator proof under ‖Dθ*‖₁=o(n) alone does not supply that denominator.

6. **CONFIRMED — Kullback gap and explicit endpoints (§§1, 4).** At the true quantile the mean check loss is σ₀=φ(zτ)/3. The negative log normalized working density therefore averages to 1+log(σ₀/t) at the optimal common scale. Subtracting mean entropy gives exactly Δ_KL. Integration yields h̄=½log(2πe)+log 2−2+π/2−log 4=0.2965876794. At τ=½ this gives Δ=0.0721558598 and ℓ₀,max=790.58235 at slack 0.03. In the order τ=0.25,0.05,0.01 the other maxima are 196.88917, 12.21853, 3.44997, with symmetric values at the upper quantiles. Use **790, 196, 12, 3.4** for conservative endpoints at that slack, or label 197 approximate and spend slightly less slack to certify 197. For the join to region (I), use its actual slack-adjusted ℓ₁ below 2t/(eσ₀) and cover from there with the grid. The nominal table entry alone leaves a gap if treated as the exact start.

7. **CONFIRMED — All-tuning interpolating-fit bound under the strict corner margin (§4).** Choose γ>0 with σ₀ exp(2γ)<2tR, then q=k(R−σ₀ exp(2γ)/(2t))/8>0. On Q(θ)<qn the lower bound nR′=n(Rₙ−8q/k) for ‖Dθ‖₁ holds. Retain m=n−3 when optimizing over the full half-line. The exact bound divided on the log scale by n is maximized at ℓ=m/(nR′), and equals (log Jₙ)/n−log t+(m/n)[log(m/(2nR′))−1]. Its limit is b*−2γ. This proves the restricted-evidence claim uniformly over s and ℓ, and the posterior claim given the denominator. An O(log ℓ/n) error cannot be discarded uniformly over the half-line.

8. **CONFIRMED — Scope of the Part 2 limitation (§6).** For fixed positive Δ_KL, the displayed Proposition N gain decays to zero as ℓ grows, so this bound gives only a constant lower endpoint for the remaining L4a assumption. Restrict the proved limitation to this bound. No universal upper bound on the improvement obtainable from every projection or a sharper conditional likelihood comparison is shown. Delete the assertion of coverage for every ℓ=o(n³) at Δ=0 unless a shrinking-margin proof controls all errors relative to nℓ⁻¹ᐟ³. In particular the displayed chi-square remainder can exceed that gain. The proposed hybrid remains conjectural.
