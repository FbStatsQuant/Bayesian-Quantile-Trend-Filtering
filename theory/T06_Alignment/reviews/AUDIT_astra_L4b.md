# Independent referee report on L4b

**Verdict — ACCEPT WITH FIXES for the Occam bounds.** At a fixed inverse scale with a strict common lower-secant margin, the unconditional lower endpoint is $n^{9/5}$ up to logarithms. The conditional endpoint is $n^{26/17}$ up to logarithms under either posterior hypothesis. The uniform U and U-σ conclusions extend to $n^{21/11}$ up to logarithms under their original assumptions. **H-sharp needs a narrower statement:** the proof gives the advertised rate at every fixed positive inverse scale under the sufficient floor in finding 8. Its assertion throughout Theorem H's original tuning range remains open. This qualification does not restrict any of the three polynomial windows.

1. **CONFIRMED — CA (§4.1).** At each positive level the two excursion sets are disjoint, and the layer-cake identity integrates independent Bernoulli sums over their runs. For a deterministic run interval with total mean $P>0$, the lower Chernoff bound at deficit $\sqrt{10P\log n}$ is $n^{-5}$. Define failure with a strict inequality, so $P=0$ never fails. There are at most $2n^2(n^2+1)$ tests. For each coordinate its sign is fixed throughout integration. In a grid cell its integrated discretization error is at most the mesh times the increase of that sign's mean. Telescoping over cells costs at most $L/n$ over all coordinates, even with endpoint atoms. This avoids needing a secant bound for the left limit at $-L$. Young's inequality charges $\eta P+(5/2)\log(n)/\eta$ per run. Integrating the initial run indicators and the positive and negative crossings costs at most $2L+TV_1$. The remaining half unit in the constant 3 absorbs $L/n$. This proves one event for all fits and all $\eta$, with failure at most $3/n$.

2. **CONFIRMED — LK (§4.2), with $C_0=36,\ C_{LK}=256$.** Put $A=\|\delta\|_1,\ B=\|D\delta\|_1$. On $w+1$ nodes, leverage gives $\max|q|^2\le9\max|q|\sum|q|/(w+1)$. A quadratic has at most two monotone stretches, so its variation is at most $4\max|q|$. On that window the Newton remainder has sup norm at most $w^2 B_W/2$ and first differences at most $w B_W$. Thus the local bound is $36 A_W/w+37w^2 B_W$. Tile the edges with windows of $w$ edges and shift the last full window flush right if there is a remainder. Every edge is covered. In this construction a shared endpoint of two earlier windows can also lie in the shifted last window only if that window starts at or before that endpoint. Its start is strictly inside the *last* previous window when there is a remainder, so the maximum multiplicity is two. Hence $TV_1\le72A/w+74w^2B$. If $3\le x=(A/B)^{1/3}\le n-1$, take $w=\lfloor x\rfloor\ge2x/3$, giving $182A^{2/3}B^{1/3}$. If $x<3$, use $TV_1\le2A\le54B$. For $x\ge n-1$, use $w=n-1$ to get $146A/(n-1)\le195A/n$ for $n\ge4$. This also covers $B=0$; $A=0$ is immediate. For smaller $n$, $2A\le6A/n$. On the **whole** Huber body, the Huber split gives $A/n\le\sqrt2r+2r^2/L\le3r$ eventually. Also $\|\delta\|_\infty\le A/n+TV_1$. With $B\le Tn/\ell$, LK gives the asserted leading bound $3C_{LK}T^{1/3}r^{2/3}k$ in the polynomial windows (the two lower-order terms fit in the slack). The logarithmic qualification is in finding 8.
