# Independent referee report on V2T

**Verdict — ACCEPT WITH FIXES.** All three conclusions at the stated lower cutoff follow for the scaled and constant positive exponential rates. The denominator needs one centre per truth, common across the tuning window. The existing mollifier gives precisely that. The corrections below preserve the proof and rates. Line references are to FABLE_V2T_proof.md.

1. **CONFIRMED — Model and uniformity (13–23, 42–46, 164–166).** In the stated L5 coordinates the normalized product law has density $(\ell/2)^m e^{-\ell\|u\|_1}$. Integration against the hyperprior gives a probability law on differences. The flat integral bound $C_k s^3$ and the finite IG moment $E s^{3-n}$ establish propriety even for an unbounded tuning support. The truncated Gamma full conditional at line 166 follows. For a self-contained statement specify a fixed interior $\tau$, define $\sigma_{0,n}=n^{-1}\sum_i E_P\rho_\tau(y_i-\theta_i^*)$, and write the UI condition with a common modulus $\sup_{n,P}n^{-1}\sum_i E_P[W_i1_{W_i>K}]\to0$ for these losses. This is the interpretation in S §1 needed for the displayed suprema.

2. **CONFIRMED — Compact scale containment (60–71, 175–176).** Both likelihood events in S §2 are data-only. The L3 exponent at radius one is largest at the lower endpoint, and the Gamma Chernoff cost on the small-loss event is at most $2^m e^{-\Lambda_-nd/8}$ throughout the support. Choose $H_0>\max(B,1)$ and $l<\min(d,1)$ as in S after fixing the evidence exponent. The claim actually needed here concerns the joint posterior's scale marginal.

3. **CONFIRMED — Window mass and statement scope (18–19, 75, 95, 143, 164).** Exponential memorylessness gives $p_{0,n}=1-e^{-b_n\Lambda_-}$. This is constant for the stated scaled rate and tends to one for fixed $b_n=b_0>0$. The two numerical values of $c_b$ round as stated. Put both schedules in line 19 and use $p_{0,n}$ through (2) and (5). More generally $-\log p_{0,n}=o(nr^2)$ suffices for both terms in (5). For a fixed positive Gamma shape and scaled rate, rescaling to $b_n\lambda$ gives the constant window mass by a conditional Gamma probability.
