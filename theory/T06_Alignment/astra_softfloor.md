# Untruncated Gamma tuning with learned scale

Throughout, Gamma uses shape and rate. Assume the audited V0T* class, a fixed interior quantile, and arbitrary fixed positive IG parameters. Write $M_n=\kappa n^{5/2}$, $t=\tau(1-\tau)$, $k=\min(\tau,1-\tau)$, and $e_n^2=n^{-5/6}(\log n)^{1/6}$. Constants can depend on the fixed class. All floors in the proof are partitions of integrals. The fitted prior has support $(0,\infty)$.

## PROVED — class-wide theorem and its adaptivity cost

For $b_n=a_n/M_n$, the full V0T* conclusions hold if $a_n\ge K n/\log n$ eventually, for a sufficiently large class-dependent constant $K$. This includes every fixed $a_n=\alpha n$, however small $\alpha>0$. Squared empirical error contracts at $O(e_n^2)$, sup error at $O(n^{-7/30}(\log n)^{1/15})$, and scale at relative error $o_P(1)$ in the posterior sense. Moreover, for every fixed $\delta>0$, the expected posterior mass of $|\lambda/M_n-1|>\delta$ tends to zero uniformly. Thus this result cannot learn a 46-fold change in the oracle multiplier.

**Proof, global evidence bound.** Use the uncentered evidence $Z_\ell$ after mixing the IG, with flat Lebesgue measure on the three orthonormal polynomial coordinates and normalized product Laplace differences. On the audited Lemma S event $\|Dy\|_1\ge nd/2, Q_*/n\le B+1$, uniformly of probability tending to one, there exists $C<\infty$ such that
\[
 \sup_{\ell>0} Z_\ell \le e^{Cn},\qquad
 Z_{\rm good}\ge e^{-Cn}p_n(J),\qquad J=[2M_n/3,4M_n/3].
\]
Here the lower bound uses the radius-one set from Lemma S §2, on $J$, and $1\le s\le2$. Its proof gives conditional prior small-ball mass at least $e^{-n}$, a unit ball in the flat coordinate, and $Q/n\le B+3+V$; the IG interval mass is a fixed positive constant. Enlarge $C$ to absorb constants. For the upper bound put $q=kd/32$. On $Q\ge nq$, reserve half the likelihood for the flat integral (at most $C_k s^3$), and maximize $s^{3-n}\exp(-nq/(2s))$ over $s>0$, which costs at most $e^{O(n)}$. On $Q<nq$, the stencil triangle inequality forces $\|D\theta\|_1\ge nd/4$. Change to ordinary trend coordinates and integrate the full ALD kernel. The result at each $s$ is bounded by
\[
 J_n(\ell/2)^{n-3}e^{-nd\ell/4}t^{-n},\quad
 J_n=\sqrt{\det(DD^T)}\le 20^{(n-3)/2}.
\]
Hadamard gives the last inequality. Maximizing in $\ell$ gives $e^{O(n)}$ since the maximum is at $4(n-3)/(nd)$. Both bounds are uniform in scale and hence survive IG integration. This gives the evidence comparison for any measurable lower tail $A$, with ratio at most $e^{2Cn}p_n(A)/p_n(J)$.

**Proof, suppression at polynomially lower tunings.** Put $H=n^{12/5}$. For $X=\lambda/M_n$ one has $P(X\le x)\le e^{-a_n I(x)}$ for $0<x<1$, $I(x)=x-1-\log x$, and the same right-tail bound for $x>1$. These follow by optimizing the Gamma MGF. Thus $p_n(J)\to1$ and $I(H/M_n)=(1/10)\log n+O(1)$. Taking e.g. $K>20C+10$ makes the posterior mass below $H$ vanish exponentially by the preceding ratio.

**Proof, remaining scales and intermediate tunings.** For the contribution from $\ell\ge H$, Lemma S §2 bounds the mass at $s\notin[l,U]$ by $o(e^{-Cn})$ for fixed $0<l<d<B+1<U$ chosen using the preceding denominator constant. Indeed on $Q<nq$ its roughness-tail proof gives $C_k 2^n e^{-Hnd/8} E[s^{3-n}]$, whose negative moment costs $e^{O(n\log n)}$. For $s>U$ use $C_k U^{3-n}$; for $s<l,Q\ge nq$ use $C_k\sup_{s<l}s^{3-n}e^{-nq/(2s)}$. Both are $o(e^{-Cn})$ by taking the endpoints sufficiently far apart.

On that compact scale interval use centered evidence $N_{1/s,\ell}=\int e^{-(Q-Q_*)/s}dc\,dP_\ell$. We claim on one further uniformly high-probability event that
\[
 \sup_{s\in[l,U],\ \ell\ge H} N_{1/s,\ell}\le \exp[O(nr^2+\log n)],\qquad
 r^2=C_r T^{2/7} n^{1/7} H^{-2/7}(\log n)^{6/7}.
\]
Here and below $T\ge4$ is fixed. To see this use C1 Steps 0–4 at the deterministic body with $T_0=Tn^3/H$. Its numerator hypotheses $H\gg n^{23/11}(\log n)^{12/11}, VH=o(n^3)$ hold. On its single event the centered loss on the body is at least $-(f/4)nr^2$ since its expectation is nonnegative. The flat section has volume at most $V_3(\sqrt n r)^3$. Outside, the audited V2T cone bound (3) is independent of $\ell$. For cone bound (4) use $n^2\|u\|_1/T_0\le G/(Tn)$ with $G=\ell\|u\|_1\sim\mathrm{Gamma}(n-3,1)$. The bound is $6V_3(\sqrt n r)^3\exp[-Tn/2+O(nr^2)+1+VH/(2n^2)+(n-3)\log2]$, by that same Gamma MGF calculation. Its exponent is negative eventually. This proves the claim, without a denominator at $H$.

On the audited V2T Step 3 event for window $J$, the centered evidence restricted to $J$ and its good set is at least $p_n(J)\exp[-O(ne_n^2+\log n)]$ simultaneously on the compact interval. This applies to the window measure itself, since Step 3 normalizes that restricted measure before Jensen. For any $A\subset[H,\infty)$ the fixed-scale posterior probability therefore is at most
\[
 [p_n(A)/p_n(J)]\exp[O(nr^2+ne_n^2+\log n)].
\]
The exponent is $o(n/\log n)$. For either fixed relative tail away from one the Gamma probability is at most $e^{-c_\delta a_n}$. Integration and the preceding two negligible regions prove the asserted pinning. Finally, condition the full posterior on $\lambda\ge 2M_n/3$. It is exactly the audited V0T* model with restricted and renormalized hyperprior and window $J$ of mass tending to one. The conditioning event has posterior probability tending to one by pinning. For any bad event its original mass is at most its conditional mass plus the removed mass. This proves all three conclusions. 

## PROVED — why broadening this Gamma does not restore class-wide oracle tracking

In the mean-calibrated family $M_n=\kappa n^{5/2}$, no shape sequence guarantees both the V0T* conclusions and class-wide posterior tracking in fixed disjoint relative windows around distinct oracle multipliers. Here tracking requires asymptotically nonvanishing posterior mass in the signal's window. The argument presumes tracking is required also on the obstruction law for a signal with finite positive oracle multiplier.

**Proof.** If $\liminf a_n\log n/n>0$, *assuming* V0T* contraction and scale consistency, pinning follows even if the constant in the sufficient theorem is too small. Restrict the numerator to the asserted trend ball of radius $O(e_n)$ and $s\in[d/2,2B]$. Lipschitzness bounds its centered integrand by $e^{O(ne_n)}$, and its flat sections have polynomial volume. Divide at each scale by the good-set bound for $J$ above. The cost is $e^{o(n/\log n)}$, so Gamma tails away from the mean vanish there; the assumed contractions discard the complement. A single limiting multiplier cannot track disjoint windows.

Otherwise take a subsequence with $a_n=o(n/\log n)$. Use the audited strict obstruction law and any admissible smooth truth: $Q_*$ is unchanged and $\|Dy\|_1/n$ changes from the zero-truth roughness by at most $V/n^3$. Write $W=[cM_n,CM_n]$ for any fixed $0<c<C$. The audited comparison of the numerator $Q\ge nq$ with a fixed interpolating rectangle $L\times S$ gives
\[
 \Pi(\lambda\in W,Q\ge nq\mid y)\le e^{-c_1 n}p_n(W)/p_n(L)
\]
on the audited events, after absorbing fixed factors in $c_1>0$. The numerator proof uses unit conditional mass at each tuning; restricting its tuning integral inserts exactly $p_n(W)$. For positive fixed $L$, the mass ratio is at most $\exp[O(1+a_n\log n)]$: integrate in $d\log\ell$, where the unnormalized density is $\ell^{a_n}e^{-b_n\ell}$; the exponential tilt favors $L$ over $W$ eventually and the log-length ratio is constant. Thus the displayed mass vanishes. A contracting trend forces $Q\ge nq$ with probability tending to one because the obstruction uses $q<\lim Q_*/n$. Tracking is impossible even when both comparison windows have tiny absolute prior mass. This also exhibits the order $n/\log n$ at which the polynomially separated windows first admit an order-$n$ log prior penalty.

## PROVED — the strict design margin alone is insufficient for candidate (ii)

There exist bounded independent periodic error laws with full secant $A2$, zero truth, and $\sigma_0<(1-\gamma)2tR$ for some fixed $\gamma>0$, for which constant shape and $b_n=c n^{-5/2}$ fail even fixed-radius contraction. The construction does not address the particular Gaussian, Beta, or mixed designs, or prescribe the size of the attainable margin.

**Proof.** First take auxiliary atomic data $Y_i=X_i+(-1)^ih$, $P(X_i=-1)=.05=\tau$, $P(X_i=1)=.95$, independently. For $0<h<1/4$, the row-average expected check loss tends to $s_*=.095$ (in fact the two shifts cancel in expectation even site by site). The limiting roughness $R(h)$ is the parity average of $E|(DX)_j\pm8h|$, with the same law of $DX$ at both parities. It is convex and continuous. $R(0)\le8E|X-1|=.8$, while $R(1/4)\ge2-.8>1$. Choose an interior crossing $R(h_0)=1$. Convexity implies $R(h)>1$ immediately to its right. At the crossing $s_*=2tR(h_0)$. Furthermore every $|(DY)_j|\ge g=\min(8h_0,2-8h_0)>0$, since stencil outputs on $X$ are even integers. The empirical roughness and loss converge to their means by bounded finite dependence.

At fixed tuning $\ell=1$ let $v= D^T\operatorname{sign}(DY)$. With sampling probability tending to one $\sum_i v_i^2\ge c_0 n$ for a fixed $c_0>0$. Indeed whenever seven consecutive $X$'s equal $1$, the four stencils at their middle site alternate in sign and $|v_i|=8$. These run indicators have positive mean and finite dependence. Always $\|v\|_\infty\le8$, $\sum_i v_i=0$.

In the exact convolution formula of astra_noFloor.md write $E_i=sU_i$ with iid unit-scale normalized ALD variables. The exact fixed-parameter evidence is $J_n(\ell/2)^{n-3}t^{-n} E\exp[-\ell\|D(Y-sU)\|_1]$, with $J_n\ge1$ by the unit minor. For real $x\ne0,z$, $|x-z|\le|x|-z\operatorname{sign}x+2|z|1_{|z|\ge|x|}$. At $\ell=1$, tilt the independent $U_i$ by $e^{s v_i U_i}$. Writing $K(z)=\log E e^{zU}$, Jensen under the tilted law gives
\[
 \log E e^{-\|D(Y-sU)\|_1}\ge-\|DY\|_1+\sum_i K(sv_i)
 -2\sum_j E_{\rm tilt}|s(DU)_j|1_{|s(DU)_j|\ge g}.
\]
For all sufficiently small fixed $s>0$, Taylor's theorem and $K''(0)=\mathrm{Var}(U)>0$ give $\sum K(sv_i)\ge s^2\mathrm{Var}(U)\sum v_i^2/4$, since the linear term sums to zero. The last sum is at most $Cns e^{-c g/s}$. Indeed for $8s<k/4$ all individually tilted laws have a common bounded exponential moment at $k/2$ in absolute value, by the ALD tails and continuity of the normalizers. Since $|(DU)_j|\le8\max |U_i|$ over four sites, exponential Markov and integration give the bound with fixed constants. Choose $s=s_1>0$ small enough that the quadratic gain exceeds the error by $2\eta n$ for some fixed $\eta>0$. This gain is at positive scale. At the crossing the limiting baseline log evidence per site is $\log(1/(2t))-1=-1-\log s_*$. Thus the interpolating evidence beats the truth benchmark by a strict exponential margin.

For precision on integration and perturbation, the same tilt argument at general $\ell$ uses $K(\ell s v_i)$ and multiplies the remainder by $\ell$. All the bounds just given hold uniformly on a sufficiently small closed positive rectangle around $(1,s_1)$, with gain at least $\eta n$ and baseline $\log(\ell/(2t))-\ell R(h_0)$ within $\eta/4$ of its crossing value (shrink the rectangle). Move $h$ slightly right and smooth the errors as follows. Independently at each site, with probability $1-p$ add an independent uniform jitter on $[-p,p]$ to the shifted atomic datum. With probability $p$ replace it by $W\sim\mathrm{Unif}[-\tau,1-\tau]$. For small $p$, the two jittered components stay outside a common neighborhood of zero, and the CDF there is exactly $\tau+px$. This gives a unique zero quantile and both secant bounds. Under this coupling the average absolute change from the crossing data is at most $|h-h_0|+O(p)+o_P(1)$ by bounded independence. For each auxiliary $U$ its change to the log integrand is at most $8\ell\|Y_{\rm new}-Y_{\rm old}\|_1$; the true check loss changes by at most the same data norm without the factor $8\ell$. These bounds preserve a positive evidence gap uniformly on the rectangle by choosing the displacement small first, then $p$ small. Meanwhile the unsmoothed displaced design has strict $2tR(h)>s_*$; convergence of the limiting means as $p\downarrow0$ preserves this strict inequality and hence some fixed fractional margin. Limits for the new empirical roughness and true loss again follow by bounded finite dependence and periodicity.

Finally take $q$ just below the new limiting true loss so the rectangle's lower exponent still exceeds $-1-\log q$. Its IG mass is fixed positive and its constant-shape Gamma mass costs only $O(\log n)$ in negative log. The audited flat-integral numerator bound on $Q\ge nq$ has exponent $-1-\log q+o(1)$. Division excludes even an empirical RMS ball of fixed positive radius around zero by Lipschitzness. This proves failure with the strict design margin.

## PROVED — extension of the tracking impossibility to arbitrary deterministic Gamma rates

Allow arbitrary positive rates, and retain the tracking requirement stated above. The small-shape mass-ratio argument already allowed arbitrary rates. On any remaining subsequence take a further subsequence along which the normalized mean $m_n=a_n/(b_n n^{5/2})$ converges in the extended half-line. If the limit is finite and strictly positive, the compact-window denominator and pinning argument above apply with a fixed window containing that limit in its interior (take a window of endpoint ratio two). If the limit is zero or infinity, no compact oracle window $W=[c,C]n^{5/2}$ can retain mass under the assumed contractions. Indeed choose a comparison window $J'=[c/4,c/2]n^{5/2}$ for limit zero, or $J'=[2C,4C]n^{5/2}$ for limit infinity. In $d\log x$, the log density at normalized tuning $x=\ell/n^{5/2}$, up to an additive constant, is $a_n(\log x-x/m_n)$. For limit zero its supremum over the target interval falls below its infimum over the comparison interval by at least $a_n[c/(2m_n)-\log(4C/c)]$. For limit infinity the gap is at least $a_n[\log2-4C/m_n]$. In both cases the prior mass ratio is at most a constant times $e^{-c' a_n}$. Use V2T Step 3 for the subprobability on $J'$: its centered good-set bound includes exactly the factor $p_n(J')$, even when that mass vanishes rapidly. The numerator on the contracting ball and compact scale interval costs at most $p_n(W)e^{O(ne_n+\log n)}$ as before. Since $a_n\gtrsim n/\log n$, division eliminates the entire target window. Subsequences cover all possibilities. The impossibility concerns actual tuning and joint class-wide guarantees; a common tuning can still give the same trend rate for many signals.

## PROVED — candidate (iii) cannot give the V0T* joint rate

For $a_n=n,b_n=1$, if the learned scale stays above a fixed positive $s_0$ with posterior probability tending to one, then the posterior probability of *any* empirical RMS ball of radius $o(n^{-1/3})$ about the truth tends to zero. Thus the squared-error and scale conclusions of V0T* cannot both hold, even for the paper designs. This is a lower bound on the attainable radius. A matching upper rate for this candidate is open here.

**Proof.** Given the trend, the tuning is Gamma of shape $2n-3$ and rate $1+\|D\theta\|_1$, independent of scale. The posterior probability of $\lambda>4n$ is bounded by $2^{2n}e^{-2n}$ by exponential Markov at $1/2$. On the remaining event and $s\ge s_0$, take $h=\lfloor n^{1/3}\rfloor$ and split off $m_b=\lfloor n/h\rfloor$ disjoint blocks of length $h$. In each block sample a fixed nonzero smooth bump compactly supported in $(0,1)$ on its $1/h$ grid, and extend it by zero, giving $w_j$. Riemann sums and three applications of the fundamental theorem of calculus give $\|w_j\|_2^2\ge c h, \|w_j\|_1\le C h, \|Dw_j\|_1\le C h^{-2}$: each third difference is bounded by $h^{-3}\sup|w'''|$, and only $h+3$ can be nonzero. Decompose the error orthogonally into these bumps with coefficients $z_j$ and a residual. Along each such coordinate, conditional on everything else including the hyperparameters, the absolute change in log posterior density per unit distance is at most $L=Ch/s_0+C(4n)h^{-2}=O(n^{1/3})$. This follows from the two Lipschitz losses; Lebesgue measure in trend coordinates has constant Jacobian. Every normalized one-dimensional log-Lipschitz density with constant $L$ has height at most $L/2$, by integrating the lower envelope $f(z)e^{-L|x-z|}$. Thus the conditional probability of $|z_j|\le u$ is at most $Lu$, uniformly. For radius $r_n=o(n^{-1/3})$, the RMS ball forces $ch\sum_j z_j^2\le nr_n^2$. At least half of the coordinates must then have $|z_j|\le C' r_n$, for a fixed sufficiently large $C'$, since $m_b h/n\to1$. The expected fraction in these intervals is bounded by $LC'r_n$ conditional on the hyperparameters. Markov bounds the ball probability by $2LC'r_n=o(1)$; integrate and add the two exceptional hyperparameter masses. V0T* would force both $s\ge d/2$ and an RMS radius $O(e_n)=o(n^{-1/3})$, a contradiction.

**Scope.** These proofs use the audited C1 numerator estimates, V2T Step 3, V0T*, Lemma S, and the uncentered comparison from astra_noFloor.md. The new conclusions have not undergone independent audit. Whether constant shape works on any particular paper design remains open.
