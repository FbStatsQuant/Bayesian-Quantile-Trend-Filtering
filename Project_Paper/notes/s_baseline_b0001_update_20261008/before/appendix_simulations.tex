\section{Additional Simulation Results}
\label{app:simulation_results}

This appendix supplements the interior-quantile comparisons of Section~\ref{sec:sim:individual} and the joint extreme-quantile experiment of Section~\ref{sec:sim:extreme}. All tables use $n=500$ and order $k=2$.

\subsection{Interior quantiles}
\label{app:sim:individual}

This appendix reports the measures omitted from Section~\ref{sec:sim:individual}: MAD and MCIW for the varying-smoothness signal, and the complete set of MSE, MAD and MCIW tables for the smooth signal. All entries use the default order $k=2$ and $n=500$, and are averages over valid replications. MCIW is the average width of the $95\%$ pointwise posterior intervals and is not available for the ADMM point estimator.

\subsubsection{Varying smoothness}
\label{app:sim:individual:vs}
The MAD comparison (Table~\ref{tab:vs_int_mad}) agrees with the MSE comparison of Section~\ref{sec:sim:ind:vs}. Bayesian-PMALA's intervals are narrower than those of both Onizuka variants (Table~\ref{tab:vs_int_mciw}); the widths describe different posterior constructions and are not by themselves a measure of calibration.

\subsection{Joint estimation of extreme upper quantiles}
\label{app:sim:joint_extreme}

Tables~\ref{tab:vs_ext_upper_cross_nb}--\ref{tab:s_ext_upper_cross_all} count the crossings among the fitted curves of Section~\ref{sec:sim:extreme}. For two reported levels $\tau_a<\tau_b$, a crossing is an index $i$ at which $\widehat{\theta}_{\tau_a,i}>\widehat{\theta}_{\tau_b,i}$. The neighbouring count of a level adds its crossings with the adjacent reported levels, so the count at $\tau=0.97$ involves $0.96$ and $0.98$; the total count adds its crossings with all three other reported levels in $\{0.96,0.97,0.98,0.99\}$. Each pair of levels contributes at most $n$ crossings, so a count can exceed $n$. Entries average the counts over replications. The levels $0.90$ and $0.93$ of the joint fit are excluded. Every retained draw of the joint sampler is ordered at every index, so its posterior means do not cross and its entries are zero.
