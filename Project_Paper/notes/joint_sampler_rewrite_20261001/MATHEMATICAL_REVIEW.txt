   Code passes lambda_SD=0 and m=0 to draw_sigma, agreeing with the unscaled prior.
   This is a working posterior construction, not a new normalized likelihood

2. Latest-neighbor conditional sweep: PASS.
   Lower neighbor is the completed current-sweep state, including ASIS; upper

3. ADMM equations: PASS.
   The smooth gradient is W(theta-r)+M(theta-x)/gamma. Adding the two augmented
   equality penalties gives A_B=W+M/gamma+rho*D'D+zeta*I. Subtracting A_B*x gives
   the increment RHS printed in A.10. Soft threshold lambda/rho and componentwise
   projection solve the two separable auxiliary subproblems. Returned b=t is
   feasible. Cold starts and fixed budget define a deterministic finite map.
   Actual bounded implementation does not cache factors across forward/reverse
   calls; the text explicitly avoids claiming that optimization is implemented.

4. MH ratio: PASS.
   Gaussian q(y|x) has log density const-||y-T_hat(x)||_M^2/(2*delta).
   Subtracting forward from reverse gives exactly equation (22)'s norm signs.
   The determinant and scale constants cancel at the fixed block settings.
   Target density is zero outside the box; no projected or truncated proposal
   density is substituted. Deterministic finite solves affect proposal quality,
   not the conditional MH invariance argument.

5. ASIS transform: PASS.
   On null(D)'s orthogonal complement, theta_perp -> lambda'*D*theta_perp has
   Jacobian proportional to lambda'^m. Its inverse cancels the prior lambda'^m.
   The log-lambda transformation adds eta, leaving a0*eta-b0*exp(eta) plus the
   Gaussian log likelihood and support indicator. Intersecting coordinate scale
   intervals and inverting s=lambda/lambda' yields the printed eta endpoints.
   Reconstructed-support checks are explicitly documented.

6. Adaptation, warm-up, singleton: PASS.
   Warm-up is optimization and is discarded. Alpha adaptation uses the mean
   of clipped acceptance probabilities, not the realized accepted fraction;
   occurs only H<t<=B; retained delta is frozen. One quantile dispatches to the
   unchanged individual routines. Fresh singleton full trajectory/RNG parity
